# SHOP_PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS

## Overview

This project analyses the performance of an online shop from **January 2024 to June 2026**. The aim was to understand sales, customers, products, orders, payments and the impact of discounts on revenue.

## Objectives

* Analyse total revenue, orders and Average Order Value (AOV).
* Identify top-performing products and categories.
* Understand customer performance by city and segment.
* Analyse cancelled and returned orders.
* Investigate payment failures and payment methods.
* Understand how discounts affect orders and revenue.

## Data & Tools

The project used four datasets:

* Customers
* Orders
* Products
* Payments

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Databricks, SQL, Power BI / Data Studio

## Process

1. Checked and cleaned the data.

2. Removed duplicates and handled missing values.

3. Checked data types and relationships between tables.

4. Joined the four datasets using the relevant keys.

5. Created a **Revenue** column using:

   `Revenue = Quantity × UnitPrice × (1 − Discount)`

6. Analysed trends, products, customers, payments and discounts.

7. Created visualisations and developed business recommendations.

## Key Insights

The analysis focused on identifying:

* Revenue and order trends over time.
* Best-performing products and categories.
* Valuable customer cities and segments.
* The impact of cancellations and returns.
* Payment failure patterns.
* The relationship between discounts, order size and revenue.

## Recommendations

The final recommendations are based on the strongest findings from the analysis and focus on improving **revenue, customer performance and operational efficiency**.

## Conclusion

This project helped me apply data cleaning, data preparation, joining, analysis and visualisation skills to a real-world retail business problem. It also strengthened my ability to turn data into practical business insights.
